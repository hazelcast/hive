        <p>Keep navigation labels short and recognizable. Group related destinations with a section label and provide icons for a recognizable collapsed rail. Items without icons retain a truncated label when collapsed.</p>

        <p>Provide a descriptive <code>ariaLabel</code>. Native links expose their destination and active links use <code>aria-current="page"</code>.
